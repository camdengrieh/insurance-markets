Real public responses, captured 2026-10-20 with plain unauthenticated GET requests
(User-Agent insurance-markets-research/0.1), then trimmed. Nothing here is invented: every value is
as the venue returned it. Only these venues' public data is used (XO, Polymarket international).

xo-markets-list.json     GET https://api-mainnet.xo.market/api/markets?marketType=2&statuses=ACTIVE&take=50&page=1&sortBy=expiresAt&sortOrder=ASC
                         9 rows picked from the 235 rows over the five pages of that listing (page 1 for the football
                         matches, page 5 for the others): six football-match markets, two chain-shutdown markets, one
                         league-winner market. `meta` is page 1's, as returned. Removed from each row: creator, resolverAddress,
                         imageUrl, metadataUri (third-party identities and links).
xo-market-lookups.json   Replies to GET /api/markets/slug/{slug} and GET /api/markets/contract/{conditionId} for those
                         markets, including the real 404 replies: the slug in a list row can be stale and answer 404 on the
                         by-slug route, while the by-contract route returns the current slug. Removed: creator, resolver,
                         description, rules, resolution text and sources, collateral token, categories, images.
polymarket-events.json   GET https://gamma-api.polymarket.com/events?active=true&closed=false&limit=20&offset=0&order=volume24hr&ascending=false
                         4 of the 20 events. Events and markets are cut down to the fields discovery reads (ids, flags,
                         dates, outcomes, token ids, fee fields, 24 h volume). The large neg-risk event is cut from 128 to
                         its first 70 markets, with fewer fields per market. No titles, descriptions or other text.
