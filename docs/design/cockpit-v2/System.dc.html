Cockpit Dither system · v2 · 2026-10-20

Paper, one ink, two sides.

A risograph desk. Warm grey paper, a near-black ink, ultramarine for Yes and rust for No at equal weight, and every tone between made by ordered dither rather than a gradient. Dither carries atmosphere and depth; it never sits under text.

Ratios are against paper for text and against ink for the dark theme. Yes and No differ in lightness as well as hue, so they read apart without colour. Caution is a word plus a dashed border plus amber text, never a fill. The dark theme swaps paper and ink and keeps both accents; the app already has the token pairs in ui/theme.css, this maps onto them one to one.

Dither

Static tone: SVG pattern fills

A 4×4 Bayer matrix at 2 px cells, eight steps, each step containing the sparser ones. Used for depth bars, scrims behind modals, rules and empty fills. Zero network, zero images, prints and exports as vector.

Live tone: the hero canvas

One canvas drawn each frame at a low resolution and scaled with pixelated rendering. A scalar field (two moving waves plus a drifting blob) thresholded by the same Bayer matrix into three inks: paper, accent, ink. Honours prefers-reduced-motion by drawing one frame. Cell size is a tweak (3 to 10 px). In the app this is the one place that moves on its own.

Rules
  • Never under text. Text sits on paper or a flat panel.
  • Depth bars use the 12/16 step in the side's ink; width is size relative to the deepest shown level.
  • Modal scrims are the 4/16 step at half opacity over the page, so the table stays legible behind the dialog.
  • Live dither appears once per page, in the hero band. Nowhere else animates on its own.
