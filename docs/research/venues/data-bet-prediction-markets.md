# DATA.BET Prediction Markets - venue spec

Researched 2026-10-19. Tags: H = read first-hand in a primary source this session, M = secondary/inferred, L = weak/stale.

## Verdict

- Kind: B2B white-label prediction-market vertical for sportsbook operators. Not a venue, not on-chain, no public market data. (H)
- Live: yes. Launched 29 April 2026 (H, news post); feature update 8 Sept 2026 (H); docs portal last-modified 12 Oct 2026 (H, HTTP header).
- Adapter priority: **skip**. No public read API, no trade path for an outside party, no independent liquidity or consumer venue to hit. Only conceivable value is as a signal source if we became a DATA.BET operator customer, which is out of scope.

## Market model (H unless noted)

- Eight categories: politics, geopolitics, finance, technology, crypto, economy, culture, weather.
- Formats: binary Yes/No, multi-outcome "range markets with custom price brackets", and (since Sept 2026) crypto "Up or Down" markets with 5m/15m/1h/4h/daily horizons, opening with a "price to beat".
- Pricing: "Odds, position control, and settlement run automatically through internally developed pricing models." Bets placed at standard decimal odds in a sportsbook-style flow (single, express, system; max one prediction selection per combo). No order book, no AMM, no outcome tokens: it is a bookmaker-style house-priced product. (H for the quotes; the "bookmaker-style, house or operator is counterparty" reading is M, since the site never names the counterparty.)
- Claims "powered by real liquidity data" and a "liquidity module" under development; the counterparty and liquidity source are not stated anywhere I could read. NOT FOUND (searched: product page, launch post, Sept 2026 update post, docs overview). Whether prices are derived from Polymarket/Kalshi is unknown. If they are, DATA.BET is a derived/duplicate price, not an independent leg. (open question)

## Chains and collateral

- None on-chain. Fiat or crypto is whatever currency the operator's platform already supports; "Players bet in whatever currency the platform already supports, fiat or crypto"; launch post says players can bet without crypto, transactions in fiat. (H)
- Stats report `volume_usd` and `liquidity_usd` per market (H, docs), but there is no wallet, token, or contract.

## Read API

- Official but **gated** (operator credentials only). Docs portal itself is public: https://docs.data.bet/ (MkDocs Material) (H).
- Predictions overview: https://docs.data.bet/trading/predictions/overview/ (H).
- Hosts (H, docs): prediction feed `https://predictions-feed.databet.cloud` (prod), `https://predictions-feed.int.databet.cloud` (integration); token endpoint `https://api.databet.cloud/oauth/token` (prod), `https://api.int.databet.cloud` (int); MTS `mts.databet.cloud` / `mts.int.databet.cloud`.
- Feed: same spec as the DATA.BET Odds Feed; messages `event_type: "sport_event_snapshot"` with `sport_event_id`, `sport_id` (e.g. `prediction_crypto`, `prediction_politics`), `version`, `timestamp_ns`, and `payload` {fixture, markets[odds{id,value,probability,status,is_active,template}], bet_stop, ...}. `fixture.type = 2`. Market names are NOT in the feed (outcome names come from `template`, e.g. Yes/No). Transport (websocket / AMQP / HTTP stream) is not stated on the overview page: NOT FOUND (searched: predictions overview, trading-integration-auth, sport-events-content-api; the Odds Feed spec page was not read).
- Content API: `GET /feeds/1/sport-events/{id}/markets`, `.../markets/{market_id}`, `.../stats` (volume_usd per event; volume_usd and liquidity_usd per market; 30 s cache, display-only) (H). Gives names and `settlement_rules` per locale; never odds or statuses.
- Auth: OAuth2 client-credentials, Bearer token up to 1 h, no refresh; legacy x509 mTLS deprecated. Credentials are generated by an existing operator in the Sport Trading Management console or by the DATA.BET integration team (H, https://docs.data.bet/trading/trading-integration-auth/). Rate limits: not documented (H, page says ask the integration team).
- Unauthenticated probes (GET only): `predictions-feed.databet.cloud/` and `feed.databet.cloud/` return HTTP 401 "authentication required"; `api.databet.cloud/` returns 404 (H, 2026-10-19). **No public sample was obtainable, so no fixtures were saved.** The data.bet product page's demo (`demo.data.bet`) is a Thai-language betting frame with no visible market data (H) and the iFrame is operator-embedded.

## Trade path

- Operators only: `POST /bets/calculate` then `POST /bets` via Managed Trading Services (MTS), selections carry `feed_id: 1`; Risk Management Mode is mandatory, `max_bet` always enforced (H). The MTS Swagger section rendered empty in the fetched page, so request schemas were not confirmed.
- End players bet through an operator's site using the operator's account balance ("no new account or wallet required"). There is no EVM or Solana signing path. (H)

## PayBox reachability

- **No.** There is no on-chain contract, signed-order endpoint, or public wallet-based onboarding. A PayBox EVM/Solana wallet cannot trade this venue via transaction proposals or signed orders. Only route is a commercial B2B operator agreement, plus an operator's KYC'd account at a licensed sportsbook, which breaks our automation model.

## Fees

- NOT FOUND (searched: product page, launch post, Sept 2026 post, predictions overview). Pricing is house-set decimal odds, so any margin is embedded in the odds; the "VWAP", "slippage-aware limits" wording in the launch post (H) suggests some size-dependent pricing but no formula is published. Commercial terms to operators are not public.

## Resolution

- Automatic settlement from "settlement rules" published per market before opening (e.g. example rule: resolves Yes if a stated price is reached on a major exchange before a UTC deadline) (H, content API doc). The data source/oracle for non-price events (politics, culture) is not stated. Dispute process: NOT FOUND. Risk: centralised operator resolution; the operator is the counterparty, so there is no on-chain dispute mechanism. Product claims GLI testing/certification (H, vendor claim, not a licence).

## Geo / ToS / automation

- Jurisdictions and licences: none listed on the site (H). Access is via licensed operators, so geography is operator-dependent. Named B2B partners on the home page for the company overall (not necessarily for predictions): GGBet, Entain, Hollywoodbets, Vulkan Vegas (H, vendor claim). Terms of Use were not read. Automated trading by third parties is not contemplated by the product.

## Liquidity

- No volume, OI or TVL published anywhere I could read. The launch post says event pages show "volume data" but gives no numbers; the Sept 2026 post also gives none (H, 2026-04-29 and 2026-09-08 posts). NOT FOUND (searched: product page, both news posts, docs). Treat as unknown and presumably small versus Polymarket/Kalshi (L).

## Event overlap for arbitrage

- Categories overlap broadly with other venues: crypto price (BTC, ETH, Gold, Oil Up/Down at 5m to daily horizons and threshold markets), finance, politics, economy. These would be arbitrage-relevant in principle, but we cannot read prices, so it is moot.

## Shared liquidity

- Unknown. The "liquidity module" and "real liquidity data" wording is consistent with either an internal book or hedging/referencing external venues. Do not treat as an independent leg without confirmation. (open)

## Suspicious content

- None found (no instructions aimed at AI agents in pages read). The launch post lists a named PR contact email; deliberately not copied here.

## Not found

- Public market-data endpoint, websocket details, rate limits, fee formula, resolution oracle/dispute process, liquidity counterparty, volume figures, Terms of Use text, MTS Swagger schema.
