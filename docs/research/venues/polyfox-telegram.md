# Polyfox (Telegram) - venue spec

Researched 2026-10-19. Tags: H = read first-hand in primary source this session, M = secondary/inferred, L = weak/stale.

## Verdict

- Adapter priority: **skip** (as a venue leg). Polyfox is a Telegram front-end / broker for Polymarket. It has no markets, books, prices or resolution of its own. All liquidity IS Polymarket's CLOB (duplicate liquidity, not an independent arbitrage leg).
- No public read API and no documented trade API. Nothing to integrate.
- Only residual value: (a) a copy-trading "smart wallet" signal source (not exposed via API), (b) builder-code attribution on Polymarket (a way to measure its flow via Polymarket's own data API).

## Kind and market model (H)

- kind: broker-or-frontend. "Telegram trading terminal for Polymarket" (https://polyfox.co/docs/legal/ states "independent trading terminal built for use with Polymarket... not affiliated with" Polymarket).
- Market model: identical to Polymarket: binary YES/NO outcome shares priced 0-100c paying $1 on win; central limit order book (CLOB). Docs: "Shares are priced in cents (0-100c) and pay $1 if the outcome wins"; limit price range 0.001-0.999. (https://polyfox.co/docs/markets/, /docs/limit-orders/)
- Also "Auto Trade" rule engine on recurring crypto UP/DOWN rounds (Polymarket short-interval markets). (H, /docs/getting-started/)
- Outcome tokenisation: Polymarket's (conditional tokens on Polygon); not described by Polyfox, inherited. (M)

## Chains and collateral (H)

- Trading wallet: normal Polygon wallet created by the bot, plus "linked Polymarket deposit wallet". Private key exportable. (https://polyfox.co/docs/security/, /docs/deposits/)
- Deposits accepted: USDC or USDT on Polygon, OP, Base, Ethereum, BSC, Arbitrum; min $10; non-Polygon deposits go through Polymarket's own bridge; auto-converted to pUSD (Polymarket's dollar token, 1:1). Polyfox deposit fee: none. Unsupported token/network = unrecoverable. (https://polyfox.co/docs/deposits/, /docs/what-is-pusd/)
- Withdrawals settle on Polygon only; asset converted back via Polymarket bridge (bridge provider fees may reduce amount); gated by optional funds password. (https://polyfox.co/docs/withdrawals/)
- Solana not supported.

## Trade path

- No programmatic/API path for third parties. Orders are placed through Telegram bot UI (buttons / chat commands) by a human; copy-trade and Auto Trade run server-side under signing permissions the user grants. (H, /docs/security/)
- Underlying venue is Polymarket CLOB with builder attribution (builder code above). An agent that wants this exposure should trade Polymarket directly (signed CLOB orders, EIP-712) rather than via Polyfox.
- PayBox reachability: **no** for Polyfox itself (no API; custodian-style bot wallet generated by Polyfox, though key is exportable). An EVM wallet reachable via PayBox could in principle send USDC/USDT to the user's Polyfox deposit address, but that gives manual Telegram-only trading, not agent-driven. Not recommended; use Polymarket directly.
- paybox_reachable: no.

## Fees (H, https://polyfox.co/docs/polymarket-fees-explained/, /docs/faq/)

- Flat 1% of trade notional on every buy and sell (including copied trades), charged by Polyfox on top (example: $10 buy costs $10 + $0.10). Paid by trader.
- No subscription, no deposit fee, no Polyfox withdrawal fee (bridge provider fees may apply); gas covered by a relayer.
- Polymarket's own fees are separate and "set by Polymarket"; Polyfox does not state the formula.
- Market orders: slippage guard default 2% buys / 10% sells, fill-or-kill. Min buy $1.
- Referral program: 25% of fees from direct invitees, 3% from theirs (H, /docs/referrals). Time-limited cashback (100% of commission, 2026-09-08 11:00 to 2026-09-09 23:59 UTC, expired) (H, /docs/cashback/).
- Effect for arbitrage: adds 1% each way versus trading Polymarket directly, so strictly dominated.

## Resolution mechanism and dispute risk

- Inherited from Polymarket (UMA optimistic oracle per Polymarket; Polyfox page: https://polyfox.co/docs/how-polymarket-markets-resolve/). Polyfox auto-redeems winning positions on-chain. Dispute risk = Polymarket's. (H for the fact it is inherited; the UMA details are M and belong to the Polymarket spec.)
